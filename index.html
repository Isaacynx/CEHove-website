About Us

About Christ Embassy

Believers' LoveWorld Incorporated, (a.k.a Christ Embassy) is a global ministry with a vision of taking God's divine presence to the nations of the world and to demonstrate the character of the Holy Spirit. This is achieved through every available means, as the Ministry is driven by a passion to see men and women all over the world, come to the knowledge of the divine life made available in Christ Jesus.

Our Vision

To Take God’s divine presence to the people and nations of the world, demonstrating the character of the Spirit.

Our Mission

Our mission is to raise generations of men and women who will come into their inheritance to fulfill God’s dream.

Our Purpose

Our purpose in Christ Embassy Church is to bring this generation of men and women into their inheritance in Christ.
